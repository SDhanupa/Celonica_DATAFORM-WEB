Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

This policy explains how Ceylonica handles information when you browse village information, create an account or contribute data.

Who is responsible

Ceylonica operates the service at ceystem.com and its authentication service at auth.ceystem.com. Contact Ceylonica at greenceylonica@gmail.com for privacy questions, complaints or requests about your information.

Account information and NIC numbers

We process your name, email address, account identifier, National Identity Card (NIC) number and mobile number as required by the registration and profile features you use. Profile completion may also ask for your address, date of birth and gender. Required fields are marked in the relevant form.

An NIC number is required for registration, including Google signup. We use it to recognize users, distinguish accounts and discourage duplicate or dummy registrations. Entering an NIC number alone does not verify a person's identity. Google does not supply your NIC number; you enter it separately. NIC numbers and personal contact details are not public listing fields.

Signing in with Google

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, we request only the OpenID, email and profile permissions. Google supplies an account identifier, your email address and its verification status, and basic profile information such as your name and, where supplied, a profile picture. We use this information to sign you in, create or connect your account with the required ownership checks, and display your account profile.

We store the account identity and relevant profile information in our authentication system and application database. Ceylonica never receives your Google password. We do not request Gmail messages, Drive files, calendars or contacts. Google account information is not used for advertising, sold to data brokers or published as part of village listings.

Contributions, surveys and location

We process the information and files you submit, including business or household information, survey answers and location details. Records may be associated with your account, village, submission time and review status. Provide another person's information only when you have the necessary authority.

If you allow browser location access, coordinates can help find a nearby village or fill a location field. Coordinates included in a submitted form may be stored with the submission. You can deny or revoke location permission in your browser.

What is public and what stays private

Village summaries and approved directory and business listings may be public. Public listing fields are limited to the listing's name or type, approved product information, registration reference and village-level context.

NIC numbers, personal contact details, individual survey answers and household GPS coordinates stay private. Authorized staff may access these records only for their administration, review, support or security responsibilities. Submitting a survey does not by itself authorize publication of its private fields. Do not put private information into a public listing name or description.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate accounts, send requested verification codes, apply permissions, save and review contributions, produce village information, answer support requests and investigate misuse or technical errors.

Hosting and backup providers process stored records on our behalf. Our SMS delivery provider processes mobile numbers and verification messages. Google processes Google sign-in requests under its own privacy policy. Map providers, including OpenStreetMap and Esri, and providers of externally loaded fonts or images receive requests that may include your IP address and the requested resource or map area. Providers may process information outside your country.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service. We do not sell personal information.

Security and browser storage

Our hosting and authentication systems process technical records such as IP addresses, request times, sign-in events and errors. We use HTTPS and account permissions to protect access. Password sign-in uses password hashes. Administrative sign-in has additional authentication requirements. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Authentication uses cookies and related browser mechanisms to maintain sessions. Browser storage can hold language preferences, the selected village and survey drafts. Drafts may also be saved on the server. Sign out on shared devices; clearing browser storage can remove unsent drafts and preferences. Never email passwords or verification codes to support.

Retention and your choices

We retain account and contribution information for as long as needed to provide the service, review contributions, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. Security records and backups may remain for operational recovery or investigation. There is currently no single automatic deletion period covering every record type.

You can request access, correction, account closure or deletion by emailing greenceylonica@gmail.com. Tell us which account or records your request concerns; do not email your full NIC or a password. We may ask for proportionate proof of account ownership through a secure process. We will explain any information we need to retain and the handling of copies in backups. Deletion is handled by request and is not an instant self-service action.

You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections settings. This does not automatically delete your Ceylonica account or previous contributions. Arrange another sign-in method first if you need continued access, and contact us separately to request deletion.

Information about children

Do not submit a child's private information without the necessary authority and any consent required by applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been collected or disclosed improperly so that we can investigate and address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Before using Google account information for a materially different purpose, we will provide notice and seek any required consent.
